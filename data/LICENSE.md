Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Exceptions:

- `geo/base/` — Natural Earth, public domain.
- `media/files/` — each file's licence is recorded in `media/media.yaml`.
